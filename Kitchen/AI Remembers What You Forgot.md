# AI Remembers What You Forgot

## Idea
The real value of an AI assistant in meal planning is recall, not invention: given your inventory and preferences, it surfaces the meals you already know but did not think of.

## Definition
Hand a chatbot your ingredient list, dietary pattern and the dishes you actually cook, and its best output is rarely a novel recipe; it is "you have frozen spinach, paneer and a tomato: palak paneer," the dish you know cold but that did not click in the moment. Used this way the AI is an unsticking tool that organises ingredients into meals, and you still filter everything through your own taste, because knowing what you genuinely like to eat is the one input it cannot supply. The trick predates chatbots: reverse recipe search engines like SuperCook have matched pantries to recipes since the late 2000s; large language models just added preferences, constraints and conversation to the lookup.

## Source
Reverse-recipe-search antecedent in SuperCook (late 2000s); the chatbot form arrived with ChatGPT's late-2022 release and was mainstream by 2023. Mined from the Green Spice Diaries meal-planning and AI-prompts videos.

## Compass

**Roots:** *where this comes from*
It slots into step two of [[Shop Last, Not First]], after the inventory and before the list, and it works best when [[Pantry as Infrastructure]] has stocked the staples that make most suggestions completable.

**Paths:** *where this leads*
Specific prompts beat generic ones, so the practice pushes you to articulate your own preferences, snacks and no-go foods, which is itself a useful audit; the output then feeds straight into [[The Sunday Reset]]'s plan.

**Neighbors:** *what lives nearby*
It is the digital cousin of asking an experienced cook "what should I make with this," and it lives most nearly beside [[Leftover Makeover]], where the same "what can this become" question is asked of a half-bowl instead of a pantry.

**Clash:** *what pushes against this*
Treating the AI as the decider rather than the rememberer produces competent, joyless menus, since it optimises what you told it rather than what you crave. It also hallucinates confident nonsense on occasion, so quantities and food-safety claims still need your judgement.
