# Two-Cook-Window Strategy

## Idea
Two deliberate cooking sessions a week beat both daily cooking and a single giant Sunday prep, because they balance effort against how fast food actually degrades.

## Definition
Rather than cooking every evening or prepping seven days at once, the week gets two cooking windows — typically Sunday and Wednesday. Each produces three to four days of components: cooked grains, a legume, a sauce, roasted vegetables, a protein. The reasoning is practical on both sides. One weekly session means food is five or six days old by the end and eaten with no enthusiasm; daily cooking means the decision and clean-up cost lands seven times. Two windows keep everything within its good window while paying the setup cost twice.

## Source
Taught as a system by plant-based meal-prep creators — notably beextravegant, who builds a course around it — and consistent with standard refrigerated food-safety windows of three to four days.

## Compass

**Roots** — *where this comes from*
It is [[Mise en Place]] applied to a week instead of a meal, and it exists because of a hard physical constraint — most cooked food is genuinely good for three or four days, not seven.

**Paths** — *where this leads*
It reframes meal prep as *component* prep rather than assembling identical boxed meals, which is what stops prepped eating from becoming monotonous and feeds directly into [[Cook Once, Eat Twice]] and [[Leftover Makeover]]. It also makes the midweek window the natural home for a quick real dinner built from Sunday's work.

**Neighbors** — *what lives nearby*
It sits beside [[The Sunday Reset]], which is the ritual half of the same idea, and beside the [[Pantry as Infrastructure]], since components only combine well if the shelf-stable layer is already there.

**Clash** — *what pushes against this*
Two windows means two clean-ups, and for anyone whose midweek is the chaotic part the second window is exactly the one that collapses. Some foods also refuse the model outright — anything where [[Crisp Is the Absence of Water]] is the point does not survive storage, and a broken [[Emulsion]] does not reheat.
