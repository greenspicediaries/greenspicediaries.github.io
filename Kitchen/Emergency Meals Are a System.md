# Emergency Meals Are a System

## Idea
The night you are too exhausted to cook is not the night to decide what to eat; a fallback meal only beats takeaway if it was chosen, stocked and frozen in advance.

## Definition
An emergency meal is a pre-decided if-then: if it is 7 PM and I have nothing left, then it is the frozen gyoza with the two-minute sauce, or the masala burger, or the labelled curry portion in the freezer. The psychology is the load-bearing part. Implementation-intention research (Gollwitzer, 1999) shows that decisions made calmly in advance roughly double follow-through, because the depleted moment only has to execute, not choose, and takeaway wins precisely when choosing is the hard part. The practice is older than the theory: *Once-a-Month Cooking* (1986) systematised the stocked freezer for exactly these nights. The system needs three things: meals you actually crave, five-minute assembly, and restocking treated as seriously as eating.

## Source
Pre-decided fallbacks per Peter Gollwitzer, "Implementation Intentions: Strong Effects of Simple Plans," *American Psychologist* (1999); freezer-meal systematisation per Wilson and Lagerborg, *Once-a-Month Cooking* (1986). Mined from the Green Spice Diaries Trader Joe's dinners video.

## Compass

**Roots:** *where this comes from*
It is the crisis wing of [[Planning Is Freedom]], the same pre-deciding logic aimed at the week's worst night instead of its best, and it rests on [[Pantry as Infrastructure]]: the freezer tier is infrastructure for evenings when assembly is all you have.

**Paths:** *where this leads*
A trusted fallback removes the guilt spiral around "failing" to cook, which supports [[Consistency Over Intensity]]: the week stays on pattern because the bad night was budgeted for. Upgraded frozen staples (a sauce from [[Flavour Cubes]], a yogurt dip) make the fallback feel like food rather than defeat.

**Neighbors:** *what lives nearby*
It lives beside [[Cook Once, Eat Twice]], whose frozen second portions are the best emergency stock, and beside [[Leftover Makeover]], the improvised cousin for nights when the freezer was not restocked.

**Clash:** *what pushes against this*
A fallback that is too pleasant becomes the default, hollowing out the cooking it was meant to protect, and leaning on bought frozen items reopens the processed-food question that [[Healthy Is Not a Permission Slip]] polices. The system works only while the emergency stays exceptional.
