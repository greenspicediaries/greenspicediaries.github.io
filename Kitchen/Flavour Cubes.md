# Flavour Cubes

## Idea
Freeze a flavour base in ice-cube trays and each cube becomes a portion, a preservative and a chiller in one: it cools the dish while flavouring it, with zero dilution.

## Definition
Any concentrated base survives the tray: muddled berry and maple for a lemonade, blended watermelon and peach for a mojito, pesto, herb chutney, leftover stock, even coffee for iced coffee. The cube solves three problems at once. Portioning: two or three cubes is a glass, no measuring. Preservation: a base that holds two days in the fridge holds months frozen. And the quiet genius, anti-dilution: a normal ice cube waters a drink down as it melts, but a cube made of the drink's own base melts into more flavour. The pattern is old home-freezer economy, mid-century magazine lore for stock and leftovers, with Marcella Hazan's frozen pesto as its most cited ancestor.

## Source
Mid-20th-century home-freezer practice, no single inventor; Marcella Hazan's Italian cookbooks taught freezing pesto (cheese added after thawing), and the tray-portion form became standard in the blog era. Mined from the Green Spice Diaries summer drinks video.

## Compass

**Roots:** *where this comes from*
It is [[Cook Once, Eat Twice]] shrunk to the smallest useful unit, and it leans on the same logic as [[Pantry as Infrastructure]]: a freezer tray of ready flavour is infrastructure measured in cubes.

**Paths:** *where this leads*
Cubes make single servings viable, which batch cooking otherwise handles badly: one glass of a drink, one portion of chutney for a sandwich, one knob of gravy base for a lone curry, extending [[Build, Brighten, Balance]] down to meals for one.

**Neighbors:** *what lives nearby*
It lives beside [[Leftover Makeover]], since the tray is the dignified fate of a half-cup of anything good, and beside the make-ahead bases whose fridge life [[Oil Separation Is a Signal]] certifies.

**Clash:** *what pushes against this*
Freezing is not neutral: raw herb brightness dulls, dairy can split on thawing, and cheese goes into pesto after, not before. Trays also go stale in a different way, as unlabelled mystery cubes accumulate, so the system needs the same curation a pantry does.
