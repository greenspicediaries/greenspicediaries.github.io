# Build, Brighten, Balance

## Idea
Prepping three versatile components, a rich base that builds the meal, an acid-fresh element that brightens it, and a cooling element that balances it, beats prepping finished meals, because components recombine and boxes repeat.

## Definition
The Indian home version is concrete: an onion-tomato gravy base (the builder) becomes chickpea curry, paneer curry or tofu mutter; a cilantro-mint chutney (the brightener) transforms sandwiches, wraps and chaat; a spiced yogurt (the balancer) works as dip, dressing and side. Each keeps roughly a week, and the week's meals become ten-minute assemblies instead of cooking sessions. The structure is what matters, not these three recipes: every food culture can fill the builder, brightener and balancer slots from its own repertoire. Full-meal prep answers "what will I eat Thursday" on Sunday; component prep answers "how fast can I make whatever I want Thursday," which survives changed appetites.

## Source
Professional mise en place and mother-sauce logic translated to home prep; the clearest print milestone is Cassy Joy Garcia's *Cook Once, Eat All Week* (2019), with the British curry house "base gravy" as the commercial ancestor of the builder. ## Compass

**Roots:** *where this comes from*
It is [[Mise en Place]] stretched from one dish to one week, and its gravy base is only shelf-safe because [[Oil Separation Is a Signal]] was obeyed when it was made.

**Paths:** *where this leads*
Components are what let [[Cook Once, Eat Twice]] escape monotony, since the second meal need not resemble the first, and single portions scale down through [[Flavour Cubes]]. A ready builder is also how a 20-minute weeknight curry like chawli chi usal becomes plausible.

**Neighbors:** *what lives nearby*
It lives beside [[Two-Cook-Window Strategy]] as the what to that strategy's when, and beside [[Pantry as Infrastructure]]: the pantry is the permanent layer, components are the weekly layer, and dinner is assembled where they meet.

**Clash:** *what pushes against this*
Anything that must be crisp resists the system, since [[Crisp Is the Absence of Water]] and a week in the fridge are enemies. And a base built for versatility is seasoned for nobody, so dishes made from it need a finishing layer of specific intent or everything starts tasting like the same Sunday.
