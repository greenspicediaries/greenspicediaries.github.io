# Film Once, Publish Twice

## Idea
One cooking session, filmed with both formats in mind, yields a long-form video and a short cutdown — so the expensive part of production is paid once.

## Definition
The costly part of a recipe video is not editing but the cook itself: planning, shopping, prepping, setting up, cooking, lighting, cleaning. That cost is identical whether one asset or three come out of it. Filming with both outputs in mind — capturing a few extra close-ups, a tighter opening, a clean hero shot — converts a single session into a long-form recipe video plus one or more short cutdowns. The constraint is that this has to be decided before filming, because the extra coverage cannot be recovered afterwards.

## Source
Standard practice in multi-platform creator workflows; the same leverage logic as content repurposing generally.

## Compass

**Roots** — *where this comes from*
It is the content instance of the same leverage that drives [[Cook Once, Eat Twice]] — pay the setup cost once, get two outputs — and it depends on [[Mise en Place]] thinking, since the decision has to be made upstream of the work.

**Paths** — *where this leads*
It makes a weekly recipe slot sustainable in a way that filming shorts and long-form separately is not, and it shapes the cadence: one proper cook per week can carry both the long-form and the short-form obligation. It is also what makes [[Consistency Over Intensity]] affordable rather than aspirational.

**Neighbors** — *what lives nearby*
It sits beside [[The Reframe Is the Hook]] as the other production-level decision — one determines how the piece is packaged, the other how many pieces exist — and it rhymes with [[Leftover Makeover]], where the second life of a thing is better than the first use of a new one.

**Clash** — *what pushes against this*
A short cut down from long-form is usually worse than one shot natively for the format, because the pacing, framing and hook structure genuinely differ. Filming for two outputs also slows the cook and adds decisions during the one part of the process that most needs attention on the pan.
